HALDER — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to HALDER: Direct Mandi & Kisan ("HALDER", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application, website, and agricultural marketplace services in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023 of India.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, phone number, role (Farmer, Retail Buyer, Wholesale Buyer, Delivery Partner), and state/district.
  • Farmer & Land Details: Farm location, crop categories, harvest date, and voluntary KYC verification documents.
  • Camera & Photo Uploads: Photos taken for crop disease diagnosis, product listing creation, and proof of delivery / dispute evidence.
  • Location Data (Approximate & Precise): Used strictly with your explicit permission to identify nearest APMC mandis and calculate accurate delivery transit distance. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Transaction & Order History: Records of items purchased, prices, delivery addresses, and dispute filings. Payment card/UPI secrets are processed directly by certified payment gateways (PhonePe / Paytm) and are never stored on HALDER servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To connect farmers directly with buyers without middlemen.
  • To calculate accurate delivery charges and dispatch routes.
  • To provide live regional APMC mandi prices and 5-day agro-weather advisories.
  • To diagnose crop diseases and suggest remedial advisories via the camera scanner.
  • To prevent fraud, verify accounts, and resolve dispute grievances.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) for all data in transit. We enforce strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and Insecure Direct Object Reference (IDOR) barriers to guarantee that no user can access another user's personal details or financial records.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We share data only with:

  • Assigned logistics delivery partners for order delivery.
  • Certified payment gateways for secure payment processing.
  • Law enforcement agencies when strictly mandated under Indian law.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to inspect, update, or request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. To exercise your rights, contact our Grievance Officer.
